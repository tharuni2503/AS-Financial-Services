Privacy policy.

How AS Financial Services and Solutions collects, uses and protects information you share with us.

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city, loan requirement, employment details and any message you choose to share.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, understand your loan requirements, contact you by phone/SMS/WhatsApp/email, and provide guidance on suitable loan options. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing of information

With your consent and as needed to assist your enquiry, we may share relevant details with banks/NBFCs or partners involved in processing your loan request. We share only information necessary for that purpose.

4. Data storage & security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Some website features (such as demo/admin tools on this site) may store data locally in your browser; please avoid submitting sensitive information on shared devices.

5. Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your contact information, or opt out of future communications, by contacting us using the details below.
